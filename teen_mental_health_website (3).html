About This Project

This website was created as a CLC 12 Grad Project. The goal is to make mental health information easier for teenagers to understand. Instead of using complicated medical language, this site explains common issues in a simple and practical way.

Main Goal: To help students recognize stress and anxiety, learn basic coping strategies, and know where to find support when they need it.

Understanding Stress

What is stress?

Stress is the pressure people feel when they have too much to handle. For students, it can come from school, exams, family expectations, friendships, or future planning.

Common signs

Stress can cause headaches, tiredness, trouble sleeping, low motivation, or feeling irritated more easily than usual.

What can help?

Small habits like planning tasks, taking breaks, sleeping enough, and talking to someone can make stress feel more manageable.

Understanding Anxiety

What is anxiety?

Anxiety is a feeling of worry or fear. It becomes a problem when it happens often or makes daily life harder.

How it can feel

Some people feel nervous, overthink situations, avoid certain tasks, or feel physical symptoms like a fast heartbeat.

Support matters

Anxiety is not a weakness. Asking for help from a trusted adult, counsellor, doctor, or friend can be an important first step.

Healthy Coping Strategies

Sleep: Try to keep a regular sleep schedule and avoid staying up too late too often.
Break tasks down: Large assignments feel less stressful when they are divided into smaller steps.
Move your body: Walking, stretching, or playing sports can help release stress.
Talk to someone: Sharing what you are dealing with can make problems feel less heavy.
Limit overload: Too much social media, school pressure, or comparison can make mental health worse.

Student Self-Check Quiz

This quiz is not a diagnosis. It is only a simple self-reflection tool.


1. How often do you feel overwhelmed by school or personal responsibilities?

2. How often do you have trouble sleeping because of stress or worry?

3. When you feel stressed, do you usually know what to do?

Helpful Resources

School Support

Students can talk to a school counsellor, teacher, or trusted adult if they need support.

Canadian Mental Health Association

CMHA provides mental health information and support resources in Canada.

Emergency Help

If someone is in danger or may hurt themselves, they should call emergency services or speak to an adult immediately.
